# Code of conduct

Codex Mods is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews) is expected to make that easy for each other.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about the work, never about the person.
- Be welcoming to newcomers. Answer the question that was asked, and point to the docs that would have answered it.
- Give and take review well: be specific, explain why, and accept that the project's maintainers decide what is merged.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Never post someone's personal data, credentials, or the content of their chats.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery, in public or in private.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure on someone to keep engaging after they asked you to stop.
- Publishing a security issue before it is fixed (report it privately: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's private reporting channel ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) has the link), and say it is
about conduct. The maintainers review every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and may edit or remove contributions, or
restrict or ban someone from the project, for behavior they find unacceptable. These rules apply in every project space, and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.
